The Lore of Mordor

Mordor

A land shrouded in darkness and ash. Home to the Dark Lord Sauron and his armies.

Minas Ithil

Once a shining city, now corrupted and renamed Minas Morgul, home of the Nazgûl.

The fiery volcano where the One Ring was forged and evil reigns eternal.
